# Gabriel Falis — Portfolio Product Context

## Register

Brand.

## Users

- App Store customers looking for support, privacy information, or terms for Gabriel's apps.
- Potential collaborators and clients who want to understand what Gabriel actually ships.
- Product and engineering peers who may follow the work or get in touch.

## Product Purpose

This site is Gabriel's credible public home: a concise introduction, a directory of real products, and a dependable place for app support and legal information. It succeeds when a visitor can understand Gabriel's focus, identify the right app, reach its App Store page when available, and find support or policies without hunting.

## Brand Personality

Quietly confident, product-minded, practical, and human. The work carries the personality; copy does not inflate it.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated portfolio language and vague claims.
- Repeated pastel card grids, glass panels, glowing blobs, and decorative gradients.
- Fake metrics, testimonials, clients, or polished case-study theatre without evidence.
- Repeated eyebrow labels and sections that exist only to make the page longer.
- Calling unfinished apps launched or linking to placeholder App Store listings.

## Design Principles

1. Proof over promise: show the actual products and their status.
2. One visual idea: editorial black-and-paper framing with product artwork as the colour.
3. Restraint with specificity: short copy, real features, clear destinations.
4. Trust is part of the product: support, privacy, and terms stay visible and readable.
5. Small screens are first-class: calm hierarchy, large targets, no horizontal overflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast, minimum 44px interactive targets, readable legal text, and reduced-motion behaviour. Colour is never the only status indicator.
